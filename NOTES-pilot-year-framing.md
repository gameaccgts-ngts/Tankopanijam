# Pilot-year framing — suggestions, not applied

**Status:** proposed only. Nothing below has been made. Raised 2026-09-21, deliberately deferred.

## The context

2027 is the **pilot year** of TankopaniJam. Scholarships are **not active** for it, but the
intent is to **build toward** a scholarship fund over time.

Right now the materials handle this by omission — scholarships simply aren't mentioned to
students. That's accurate, but it leaves two soft spots:

- The Students card offers "National bragging rights for winning" with nothing behind it,
  which can read as a thin prize rather than a deliberate first-year position.
- The sponsorship prospectus refers to "the scholarship fund" once, with no explanation of
  what that fund is or why it doesn't pay out yet.

Saying "pilot year" out loud fixes both. It turns an absence into a reason to get in early,
and it turns the sponsor ask into founding something rather than topping up an existing pot.

## Suggested edits

### 1. University one-pager (`TankopaniJam-university-one-pager.docx`)

Add a line to the opening paragraph, after the "seventy-two hours" sentence:

> 2027 is the pilot year — the first cohort of five schools.

Rationale: for a department head, being in the founding cohort is a stronger draw than
anything we can currently offer as a prize.

### 2. Sponsorship prospectus (`TankopaniJam-sponsorship-prospectus.docx`)

Replace the second paragraph under **What sponsorship does**:

> Current: "Funds support Child Life gaming at the bedside and the scholarship fund, and
> cover the cost of running the event for the students — meals, equipment and on-site needs."
>
> Proposed: "2027 is the pilot year. Funds cover the cost of running the event for the
> students — meals, equipment and on-site needs — support Child Life gaming at the bedside,
> and begin building the scholarship fund that future cohorts will compete for. Sponsors of
> the pilot are founding it, not topping it up."

Consider also a small line on the cover, under the dates:

> Pilot year — the first TankopaniJam.

### 3. Website Students card (`index.html` + `index-neon.html`)

Optional. Either add a fifth bullet, or extend the card body:

> 2027 is the pilot year — you'd be the first cohort.

**If this is done it must be mirrored to both landing variants**, then verified with the
copy sync check (strip tags, word-diff; the scroll-cue glyph is the only expected difference).
Note that the copy deck currently out with a coworker predates any such change, so reconcile
before applying returned edits.

## Deliberately not suggested

- Adding scholarship language back to anything student-facing. The pilot has no scholarship
  to award, and implying otherwise is the thing we removed.
- Touching the deck under `pages/`, which still lists scholarships as an active prize
  (including "Patients' Pick — wins a scholarship"). That's legacy PowerPoint copy and is
  not authoritative, but it will need reconciling whenever the deck is swept.
