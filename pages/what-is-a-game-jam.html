TankopaniJam · aka the Nemours Game Jam

What is a Game Jam?

A 72-hour creative marathon where college teams design, build, and rapidly prototype a game within a strict timeline, based on a specific set of requirements set by the organizers.

⚙️ How it works

  • College teams
  • 1 theme / game prompt, suggested by patients
  • 3 days to build a demo-quality game to requirements
  • Judged by patients!
  • Scholarship prizes for teams
  • Corporate donors fundraise through sponsorships

🎯 Why a Game Jam?

It's a fantastic way to network with other game developers, test your skills and creativity, and learn how other developers handle stressful situations.

Some ideas go on to become fully-fledged games or reveal innovative ways of developing in the future. They can benefit beginners and seasoned veterans alike!

⏱️ The "72 Hours"

A 3-day creative marathon of designing, building, and rapidly prototyping a game within a strict timeline, based on a specific set of requirements set by the organizers.

🌟 Benefits

  • Experience for game developers in college
  • Awareness for Child Life and Nemours
  • Scholarships for participants
  • Corporate donations go directly to patient interaction through gaming
  • A perfect blend of education, technology, philanthropy, fun, and purpose
