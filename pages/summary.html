TankopaniJam · aka the Nemours Game Jam

Summary

One event, five winners — the patients, the student teams, the corporate sponsors, Nemours, and the Estate.

🗂️ The Estate

All games and assets go into the file storage of the Estate for future game jams and use within the Estate preservation plan. The Estate gains game content to use as a Child Life tool and an educational tool about the Estate itself.

🧒 Patients

Patients get to play the games and judge the outcomes of the jam — and they help define the requirements, like "Create a game that helps kids deal with IV pokes," or an adventure game based on the airplane that landed on the Estate's grounds.

🎓 Student Teams

Teams get the experience of creating a game in a short amount of time, work with staff from the Nemours organization, and have the potential to further develop the game for the Estate.

🤝 Corporate Sponsors

Sponsors raise funds to support their chosen team, with those funds going directly to the patients and student teams involved in the game-development process.

🏥 Nemours

Nemours gains activity and funds for Child Life and increases awareness of the gaming & technology needs here at the hospital.
