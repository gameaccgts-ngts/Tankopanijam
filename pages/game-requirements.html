Game Requirements

Every submitted game must meet these baseline requirements.

  • A menu system capable of getting the player into and out of gameplay.
  • Music & sound effects throughout.
  • Total play time must be between 3 and 10 minutes.
  • Levels can vary.
  • Must be playable on Windows or Web.
  • Utilize Xbox controllers — added bonus for thoughts on adaptive controls.
  • Must incorporate the Estate elements and be conscientious of the judging criteria.
