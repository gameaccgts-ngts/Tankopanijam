When

Timeline

3 days to build, 1 day of rest & testing, and 1 release day.

Days 1–3Build days
  • Meet & Greet — 10am. Teams meet in the Hospital Atrium for a tour of the Child Life Clubhouse, CCTV Studio, and school programs.
  • Teams also meet up with Patient Judges to review their unique needs while gaming and why gaming was important to them while here.
  • Kick-off meeting — 11am. Game requirements and themes are set; students build from the release of requirements (approx. 11:30am) until 5pm on the 3rd day.
  • Team Sponsors are briefed in a separate meeting immediately after.
  • Each day there is a check-in requirement from each of the teams.
Day 4Rest & testing
  • One day of rest for the teams to recover.
  • Our featured streamers / content creators do a special stream with interviews, highlights, and testimonials from patients on how gaming is so important in their recovery and time spent here.
  • Judges talk about the criteria and requirements, and get a first chance to play the submitted games.
  • Patients get a "first look" at the submitted games and can play them in the atrium.
Day 5Release day
  • Release day & Judgement day.
  • Judges announce the winners in the various categories, announce the scholarships, and show highlights from the stream feedback.
