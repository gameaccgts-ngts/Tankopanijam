The heart of the Jam

Patients

Present and former patients sit at the center of TankopaniJam — defining the prompt, playing the games, and judging the results.

  • We include a panel of patients (present & former) to give specific insight on how their situations and needs around gaming were met or identified while here.
  • Any appropriate patient is allowed to play and participate in Days 3 & 4 for judging and feedback.
  • TAC can also play a part in theme development and requirements.
  • Patient video interviews and Zoom meetings can be facilitated for questions from teams.
