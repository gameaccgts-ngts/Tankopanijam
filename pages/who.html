The Teams

Who

Five teams of college students, each with their own sponsor driving fundraising.

👥 5 Teams

  • Maximum of 5 people and a minimum of 2 on each team
  • Teams are made up of students from each invited school
  • Must have their own laptop / workstation
  • Can use any game engine, but Unity is preferred
  • Must be physically present at the kick-off meeting and the submission meeting
  • All assets, code, scripts, and materials have shared ownership with the participant and Nemours Estate — see legal page

📣 Team Sponsor

In charge of fundraising for the team. A sponsor can be the school itself or a corporate sponsor.

Each school sponsor is responsible for at least two check-ins over the three days.
