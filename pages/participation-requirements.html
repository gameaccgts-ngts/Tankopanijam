Participation Requirements

Who can be on a team, and what's expected of them and their school sponsor.

  • Must be in college, studying a related field — game development, computer science, business, etc.
  • Able to be here for a week, accompanied by a sponsor from their school.
  • One sponsor from each school (all five team members share one sponsor).
  • Each school sponsor is responsible for at least two check-ins over the three days.
  • GPA of 3.0 or higher.
