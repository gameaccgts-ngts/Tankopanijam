Check-ins

Over the three days there are two check-in sessions each day — six in total. Each session is evaluated for ranking across the three competitive build days.

Day 112pm & 4pm
  • Game Design Document — DRAFT
  • Team assignments of roles and team name
  • Vision / theme board completed
Day 212pm & 4pm
  • Complete assets list of every script, file, and document up to this point
  • Game project started, with a back-up schedule / method shown
  • Lunch-time check-in — general health of the team checked
Day 311am & 3pm
  • Demo check-in — must have some level of functional game with the menu working 50%
  • Back-up of project by 3pm
  • Lunch-time check-in — general health of the team checked
Final5pm, Day 3
  • Final submission check-in. A final build, all assets, projects, and documentation must be brought to the judges' station via the provided hard drive.
  • Judges submit all check-in paperwork in preparation for the 4th day of judging and play-throughs.
FundraisingBy event end
  • Each team needs to raise at least $200 by the end of the event.
