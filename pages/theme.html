The creative constraints

Theme

A single theme word plus a handful of Estate-rooted ingredients every game must weave in.

  • One theme word — for example, "Distraction" — demonstrated within the game through mechanics, art assets, or some other unique method.
  • 2 physical objects from the Estate that must appear in the game.
  • 1 educational aspect from the Estate (for example: how the water pumps work at the Estate).
  • 1 historical context drawn from the two physical objects.
  • A sample tune or piece of music from Alfred's collection.
  • 2D or 3D? That's up to the team.
  • Aesthetics are completely up to the team, as long as they fit the theme.

The theme and requirements can be shaped with input from the patient panel.
