Judging Day

While teams rest, the judges go to work on every submission.

  • Judges go through all submitted materials for completeness and immediately contact the team lead if there are issues.
  • Each game submitted gets evaluated by judges with the rubric provided.
  • Judges award the winner — and also get to award the Minors (Mummy, Yip, Monty, etc.).
