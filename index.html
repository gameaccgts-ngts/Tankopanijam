Why this event exists

01

A hundred years ago, Alfred I. duPont filled this place with everything he loved.

02

Then he left it to children going through medical treatment.

03

Now you get to build something for them, out of it.

TankopaniJam. The Estate opens its archive — objects, music, history — and five teams get seventy-two hours to turn it into a game for the kids who are here right now. The kids write the brief. The kids judge the result. There are three ways to be part of it.

A kid watches their own idea come to life.

And a few feet away, the student who built it watches that happen. Everything else is logistics — and the games don't get archived afterward, they stay in rotation, played at the bedside.
